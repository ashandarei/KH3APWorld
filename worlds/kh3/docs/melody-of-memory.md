# Melody of Memory music chests

Music chests let you play a song in Kingdom Hearts Melody of Memory to unlock
a chest in KH3. This is optional and must be enabled when your seed is created.

## Set up Melody of Memory

You need the **Epic Games Store version of Melody of Memory**.

1. Close Melody of Memory.
2. Open **KH3 Config** in the KH3 client.
3. Choose your Melody of Memory installation folder on the **MoM Dir** row.
4. Click **Install Mod** beside it and wait for installation to finish.
5. Keep the KH3 client open and connected while playing your seed.

## Open a music chest

1. Find a blue-and-gold music chest in KH3 and choose **Examine**.
2. Play and clear the selected song in Melody of Memory.
3. Return to KH3. The chest should now offer **Open**.
4. Open it to collect its reward.

Music chests also give an extra Archipelago item when opened. If an ordinary
chest was changed into a music chest, it keeps its original reward as well.

Each chest needs its own song clear, even if you have already played that
song for another chest. Failed or unfinished attempts leave the chest locked;
you can try again. Your completed chest challenges are remembered when you
return to the same run.

## Choose how many music chests to include

Use the KH3 player-options editor before generating your seed:

- **Music Chest Count:** add up to 8 extra music chests.
- **Convert Vanilla Chests to Music Chests:** change up to 128 ordinary chests
  into music chests. The actual number depends on how many chests your seed has.

Set both counts to **0** to leave music chests out of the run.

## Choose your songs

The player-options editor lets you browse songs by theme, world, and source
game or album. Different arrangements of the same song are listed separately.

- Leave the **Song Whitelist** empty to allow every song except those you exclude.
- Add songs or categories to the whitelist to use only those selections.
- Use the **Song Blacklist** to exclude songs or categories. A whitelist
  selection takes priority if the two lists overlap.

The editor shows how many songs are available and whether songs will need to
repeat. If you enable music chests, keep at least one song available. Changing
these choices requires a new seed.

## Change how you play a song

In KH3, open **Game Settings → Melody of Memory** before launching the next
song. You can choose:

- **Party:** Team Classic, Team Days, Team 3D, or Team BBS.
- **Difficulty:** Beginner, Standard, or Proud.
- **Play Style:** Normal, One Button, or Performer.
- **Starting Item:** None, Potion, Mega-Potion, or Summoning Star.

Clearing the song on your chosen difficulty and play style counts, including
an easier difficulty than the seed's starting choice. Summoning Star also
adds King Mickey to the party for that song.

If a song will not launch, check **MoM Dir** and **Install Mod** in KH3 Config,
then follow the client's error message.
