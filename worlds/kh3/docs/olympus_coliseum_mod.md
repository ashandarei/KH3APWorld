# Olympus Coliseum

The optional Olympus Coliseum mod adds tournament cups to KH3. With
Archipelago rewards enabled, each of the 24 supported cup clears gives one
Archipelago item.

## Install the mod

Use Olympus Coliseum **1.4.2 or 1.4.2.1** and the two required boss mods.

1. Close KH3 and open the KH3 client's **Third Party Mods** tab.
2. Find **Olympus Coliseum** and use its download link to get the mod.
3. Click its **folder icon** and extract the downloaded mod file into that folder.
4. Download each required boss mod using its own link and put it in its own
   storage folder, opened with its **folder icon**:
   - [Zodiac Phantom Aqua](https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomhearts3/mods/2067?tab=files): the main **Phantom Aqua** download,
     `PhantomAqua_BossBattle.pak`.
   - [Key of Destiny – Data Roxas Boss](https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomhearts3/mods/2150?tab=files): the **Roxas Boss – Coliseum Version**
     download, `RoxasBoss_ColiseumVersion.pak`.
5. Click **Restore all** to install the group. Follow any messages about
   missing files or unsupported versions.
6. Connect the client to your room and let it finish preparing your run before
   launching KH3.

Choose the Coliseum version of the Roxas download. The other Roxas versions
cannot be used for this integration. The client handles the installed filenames
and the adjustments needed for Archipelago rewards.

To put the mod aside later, close KH3 and use **Remove all**. The files are
kept in storage so you can use **Restore all** again.

## Choose your cup rewards

Set **Olympus Coliseum Mod Pool** before generating the seed:

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Off** | Cups do not give Archipelago rewards. The mod is optional; if you install it, cups keep their normal rewards. This is the default. |
| **Randomize** | Each supported cup gives one Archipelago item, which may be needed to finish the run. |
| **Junk** | Each supported cup gives one filler item (not needed to finish) or a trap. |

The hidden Sephiroth cup is not included in Archipelago rewards.

## Open the cups during your run

For Randomize or Junk, open the Coliseum menu and choose
**Settings → Unlock Features → Series**. This makes the extra series available
before the end of the game while keeping each cup's normal story requirements.

Every Archipelago cup requires finishing Olympus. The following story
completions are also needed for the listed cups:

| Required completion | Cups |
| --- | --- |
| Olympus and Twilight Town | Phil; Pain and Panic; Hermes; Chef; Flan; Terra |
| Toy Box and Kingdom of Corona | Pegasus; Cerberus; Apollo; Ventus |
| Monstropolis and Arendelle | Hercules; Titan; Athena; Aqua |
| The Caribbean and San Fransokyo | Goddess of Fate |
| Base game / Master Xehanort | Hades; Gold; Platinum; Paradox; Zeus; The Story So Far; Infernal; Limit Cut; Vessels of Darkness |

Postgame cup rewards are not required to get the items needed to reach the
final boss.
