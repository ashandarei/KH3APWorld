# Kingdom Hearts III

Play Kingdom Hearts III as part of an Archipelago multiworld. Opening chests,
finishing battles, and completing other challenges can send items to you or
another player.

The Garden of Assemblage is your hub for travelling between worlds. This
integration is based on the original
[Garden of Assemblage randomizer](https://github.com/Water-and-Critic/KH3-Rando-GoA).

## Getting started

Follow the [setup guide](setup_en.md) to install the KH3 client, prepare the
game, and join your room. The client prepares your run automatically
when you connect.

## Goal

Collect the Heart Pieces required by your seed to open the goal portal. Some
seeds also require you to finish a chosen number of worlds (setting).


## What can give rewards?

- Chests, story rewards, and victory bonuses.
- Lucky Emblems and Battlegates.
- Data Battles and minigames.
- Moogle Shop purchases and Workshop rewards.
- Optional [Melody of Memory music chests](melody-of-memory.md).
- Optional [Olympus Coliseum cups](olympus_coliseum_mod.md).

## More ways to customize your run

- [Shuffle the music or use your own songs](music.md).
- [Randomize Sora's stats, equipment bonuses, or food effects](stat-options.md).
- [Choose equipment abilities and minigame rewards](equipment-and-minigame-pools.md).

## Returning to a run or starting another

Close KH3 before changing runs. Use the kh3 client's **Save Manager** to activate the run you
want, then connect and launch the game.
