# Equipment abilities and minigame rewards

Choose these settings in the KH3 player-options editor before generating
your seed. Each minigame can have its own reward settings.

## Equipment abilities

**Equipment Abilities Pool** controls the abilities attached to your gear:

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Vanilla** | Each piece of equipment keeps its usual abilities. |
| **Randomize** | Abilities are shuffled between pieces of equipment. Each piece keeps the same number of ability slots. |
| **Junk** | Abilities become utility abilities such as Treasure Magnet, Lucky Strike, or Item Boost. |

These abilities stay on the equipment. This setting does not change equipment
stats, Keyblade upgrade abilities, or which items other players send you.

## Minigame rewards

Separate settings control:

- Flantastic Flans.
- Verum Rex.
- Festival Dance.
- Frozen Slider.
- Flash Tracer Course A.
- Flash Tracer Course B.

**Randomize** allows these rewards to contain items needed to finish the run.
**Junk** limits them to filler items (items not needed to finish) or traps.
These minigame reward settings start on Junk.

Flantastic Flans includes each flan's one-time high-score reward. Fruit rewards
stay the same. Toy Box's Strawberry Flan high-score reward needs 17,000 points.

## Choose the ranks you want to earn

Each scored minigame also has a **Maximum AP Rank** setting. It controls how
high a rank can be needed for important rewards:

| Choice | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **D, C, B, or A** | Ranks up to that choice follow the minigame's reward setting. Higher ranks give only filler or traps. |
| **Junk** | Every ranked reward gives only filler or traps. |

For example, choose **Randomize** and a maximum rank of **B** to allow
important items at D, C, and B, while keeping A-rank rewards optional.
Set both the reward setting and maximum rank to suit how much of the minigame
you want to play.

Earning a higher rank also collects the lower-rank rewards: A includes B, C,
and D; B includes C and D. Each reward can be collected once. Replaying the
same rank does not give more Archipelago items. There are no S-rank rewards.

Frozen Slider's all-treasures rewards are separate from its ranks. **Frozen
Slider Pool** controls them, but **Maximum AP Rank** does not limit them.
Flantastic Flans also has no maximum-rank limit.

Change these settings before generating a new seed.
