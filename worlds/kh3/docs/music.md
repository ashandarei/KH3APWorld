# Music and custom songs

You can shuffle KH3's music, give Keyblade forms their own themes, or play
your own music files. Choose the starting settings when creating your seed;
you can also change music settings in the game.

## Music choices

| Setting | What you hear |
| --- | --- |
| **Vanilla** | The game's usual music. Custom replacements and enabled form themes can still play. |
| **Shuffle Worlds** | Each world's exploration and battle music moves together to another world. |
| **Shuffle Battle Themes** | Exploration and battle music are shuffled separately. |
| **Chaos** | Music from different worlds and activities is mixed together. Enable **Include Custom Music** to let Chaos also choose from your own songs. |

Some scenes keep their usual music, including cutscenes and the Festival Dance,
so the music still fits what is happening on screen.

## Keyblade form themes

Turn on **Form Themes** to hear a special theme while using a Keyblade form.
The previous music returns when the form ends. Anti Form has its own theme.
Some form themes pause when you stop taking action and resume when you act again.

## Use your own music

1. Open **KH3 Config** in the KH3 client.
2. Use **Music Dir** to choose the folder containing your WAV or OGG music files.
   The **Default** button selects the mod's usual music folder.
3. To replace a particular world's music, name the file for that world. For
   example, `Olympus.ogg` replaces music in Olympus.
4. Open **TARGETS.txt** in the music folder for the other supported names,
   including specific songs and Keyblade forms.
5. Restart KH3 after adding or changing music files.

If **TARGETS.txt** is missing, play your run once with that music folder
selected, then close KH3 and check the folder again.

For a custom Keyblade form theme, use its listed name, such as
`Form_UltimaWeapon.ogg`. A custom form song can play even when automatic
Form Themes is off.

Named replacements work in every music mode. **Include Custom Music** controls
whether Chaos can pick your songs as random choices; it is not needed for a
named replacement. A custom world or song replacement takes priority over
automatic form themes while it is playing.

Your music files stay on your computer. Other players do not need copies.
